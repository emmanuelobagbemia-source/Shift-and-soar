About the founder

I spent years in the seat you're in now

I didn't come into leadership with a blueprint.

I was thrust into leadership roles early in my career with no real direction or support, and I had to figure it out as I went. Mentors helped me tremendously along the way, but the real turning point was realising that I already had the answers inside me. I just needed someone to help tease them out.

Once I started coming up with my own answers, rather than borrowing someone else's, it became so much easier to actually own my leadership journey. That's the coach I set out to become.

Now, through Shift and Soar, I specialise in coaching new and first time managers through that same transition: the ones navigating their first direct reports, their first hard conversations, their first “wait, is this actually my job now?” moment. Backed by my own leadership experience and a Level 5 Coaching Professional Qualification, I don't hand people a script, I help them find their own answers, because that's what actually sticks.

I work with individuals 1:1 and with businesses and teams, helping people move from uncertainty to clarity, and from just getting through the day to genuinely thriving in their role. Whether you're stepping into management for the first time or leading a team that's growing fast, I'm here to help you shift what's not working, and soar into what's next.

FAQ

Common questions

01How long before I see results?

Most clients notice a shift in confidence within the first month, with deeper change over a programme of 3 to 6 months.

02Do you only work with new managers?

It's the core focus, alongside team and business coaching. Senior leadership coaching is coming soon.

03What does team coaching involve?

It's scoped to your rollout, typically a cohort of newly promoted managers moving through the programme together.

04Do sessions happen in person or online?

Both. Coaching runs by video or in person, whichever suits you or your team best.
